# Affiliate disclosure

This is a drafting aid, not legal advice. Verify the live site, programme terms and current UK guidance before publishing.

## Site-wide wording

> **Affiliate disclosure:** Some links on this website are affiliate links. If you purchase through one of these links, we earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Use the programme-specific wording only after joining that programme. Amazon’s current Associates agreement requires this exact statement on the site:

> **As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.**

Do not claim to be an Amazon Associate before acceptance. Add any eBay wording required by the current EPN agreement after joining.

## Article wording

For a mixed editorial article, put this before the first affiliate recommendation:

> **Advertisement / affiliate disclosure:** This article contains affiliate links. If you click a link and make a qualifying purchase, we earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The disclosure applies to the product recommendations and links that follow.

For an article wholly focused on affiliate-linked products, label it clearly as `Ad` before the reader engages, including in the title where appropriate.

## Placement rules

- The disclosure must be clear, prominent and close to the relevant recommendation or link.
- A footer-only disclosure is not enough for content containing affiliate links.
- Do not use only “affiliate link”, “partner” or “may earn a commission”.
- Repeat the disclosure near separate product blocks when needed for clarity.
- Keep disclosures visible on mobile and before a reader needs to click.
- Do not publish a live link until the programme, ID, destination and last-checked date are in `automation/link-register.csv`.

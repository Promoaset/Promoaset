## Before applying to a programme

- [ ] Refresh the site with original, useful content rather than thin product pages.
- [ ] Add About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms and Affiliate Disclosure pages.
- [ ] Supply the real legal/trading name and public privacy email; do not use placeholders on the live site.
- [ ] Check that every product recommendation is supported by evidence and has a current disclosure.
- [ ] Review the current Amazon UK and eBay Partner Network terms before applying.
- [ ] Decide which traffic sources are allowed and avoid incentivised clicks, cookie stuffing and misleading claims.

## Before hands-off publishing

- [ ] Confirm the WordPress plan supports the required authoring access or use an approved alternative publishing route.
- [ ] Confirm the active theme, mobile layout, forms, analytics and cookie controls.
- [ ] Add approved affiliate IDs and exact links to `automation/link-register.csv`.
- [ ] Decide whether AI-generated drafts require human approval (recommended: yes until a reliable review history exists).
- [ ] Test one draft in preview before enabling any publishing action.
- [ ] Set a monthly review for broken links, price/stock changes, disclosures, conversions and stale content.

## Privacy and cookies

If analytics, forms or other non-essential storage/access technologies are enabled, audit the live configuration and provide clear information and a valid consent mechanism. Do not promise a cookie banner or tracking control that the live site does not actually have.

See [ICO guidance on cookies and similar technologies](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/cookies-and-similar-technologies/).
